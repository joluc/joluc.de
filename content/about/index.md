# Hey, I'm Jonathan! 👋

Cloud engineer with a passion for observability and signals. I work with teams to build robust monitoring systems that actually help you understand what's happening in your infrastructure. There's something deeply satisfying about turning telemetry data into actionable insights that prevent outages and improve performance.

## My Passion for Signals and Observability

Modern distributed systems generate an incredible amount of telemetry data – logs, metrics, traces, and events. My passion lies in making sense of these signals to create meaningful observability. I love the challenge of designing monitoring systems that not only detect problems but help teams understand the "why" behind system behavior.
